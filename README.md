# ⚽ Kaweera · كويرة

**Kaweera by Sheebo** is a football quiz game in English, Arabic and French. Play it in your browser: **[doudoushehab2003-create.github.io/kaweera-by-sheebo](https://doudoushehab2003-create.github.io/kaweera-by-sheebo/)**

> انت والثلاث خشبات

## What you can do

- **Solo quiz:** 4,000 questions on the World Cup, AFCON, Egyptian and Arab football, Europe, Latin America, the Ballon d'Or, legends and the Laws of the Game. Easy questions are worth 10 points, medium 20 and hard 30. You get speed and streak bonuses, and each round ends with a ⚡ flash round.
- **XI:** a pitch with the starting eleven of a famous final (300 lineups, from World Cup, Champions League, Euro, Africa Cup and more, including Egypt). Each shirt shows its number; tap one and type the player's name. XI matches use **at most two opposing teams from the same real match**, including any distinct replay; offline group games and online rooms are limited to one matched pair per challenge.
- **Daily quiz:** questions about the day's real matches. You type your answer, and it's checked against the real result online.
- **Play with friends:** teams can share one phone, or friends can join an online room with a code.
- **Live scores:** today's, yesterday's and tomorrow's matches from the competitions you pick.
- **Leaderboards:** for every level and the daily quiz.
- **Tokens and rewards:** you earn tokens for every finished game. You can also buy tokens with InstaPay: pick a pack or any amount from 10 to 5,000 EGP, at 10 tokens per EGP. Tokens can be swapped for real football rewards such as tickets, balls, shirts and boots.
- **Your account:** sign up with an email, so your scores and tokens follow you.

## How to play

1. Open the link and press **Start**.
2. Pick a level and answer before the clock runs out. Each round has 20 questions.
3. You have 3 lives. A wrong answer or running out of time costs one.
4. Sign up to save your score to the leaderboard and earn tokens.

## Built with

- [Expo](https://expo.dev) and React Native (TypeScript), so the same app runs on iPhone, Android and the web.
- Firebase for accounts, leaderboards, rooms and tokens.
- Live match data from a public scores feed.

This repository holds the published website only. The `xi-match-patch.js` guard updates the current compiled web build; these rules must also be implemented in the original Expo/TypeScript source before the next rebuild.
